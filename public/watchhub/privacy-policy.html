WatchHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information WatchHub ("the app") collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it. The app is a personal TV show and movie tracker - it helps you keep a watchlist and track which episodes you've seen.

Information we collect

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address - used to sign you in and let you recover your account.
  • Display name - shown on your profile within the app.
  • Optional personal info - birth date, gender, and country, only if you choose to enter them. These are never required.
  • Profile photos - a profile picture and cover image, only if you upload them.
  • Viewing activity - your watchlist, which episodes you've marked watched (and when), your ratings, episode moods, favorites, custom lists, and shows/movies you've marked "not interested" in.
  • Community content - comments, group posts, replies, likes, and the GIFs you attach, if you use those features. These are visible to other users.
  • Subscription status - whether you have an active WatchHub Plus subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Whether or not you have an account, the app also collects:

  • Crash reports and diagnostics - technical details when the app crashes or hits an error (device model, OS version, app version, the error itself), used only to find and fix bugs. They don't include your name or email.
  • Basic usage events - a few events such as "app opened", "signed in", or "import completed", used to understand how the app is used.
  • Advertising data - free users see ads served by Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on your IP address), and ad interactions. See "Advertising" below.

If you use the app without creating an account ("Continue without an account"), your watchlist and viewing activity stay only on your device - we have no way to access them.

How your information is stored

Your data is stored in two places:

  • On your device, in a local database, so the app works fully offline.
  • In our backend database (hosted by Supabase), if you're signed in - this is what lets your watchlist sync across your devices. Your private data can only be read or written by your own signed-in account; community content you post is visible to other users.

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to show ads, measure them, and prevent ad fraud. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. WatchHub Plus subscribers don't see ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Third-party services

  • TheTVDB - all show/movie information, posters, cast info, and episode data come from TheTVDB's API. Searching or browsing sends your search terms to TheTVDB, but not your account information. This product uses the TheTVDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TheTVDB.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) - used narrowly, only to distinguish a "Canceled" show from one that's simply "Ended" (data TheTVDB doesn't separately track) - no account information is sent, only the show's identifier.
  • Supabase - our backend provider, used for account sign-in and storing the data described above.
  • Google AdMob - serves ads to free users (see "Advertising").
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat - process and verify WatchHub Plus subscriptions.
  • Sentry - receives crash reports and diagnostics.
  • GIPHY - powers GIF search in comments; your search terms are sent to GIPHY.

We do not sell your data, and we don't share your account information or viewing activity with advertisers or data brokers. Ad-related data is collected by Google AdMob as described above.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most of your information directly in the app (Profile, Settings, and individual list/rating screens).
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account within the app.
  • If you don't have the app installed, you can request deletion here: Request Account Deletion.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including restricting database access so your data is only ever readable by your own account. That said, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
