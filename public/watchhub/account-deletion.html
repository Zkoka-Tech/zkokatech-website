Request Account Deletion

There are two ways to delete your account and all associated data from WatchHub.

Option 1 - In the app (fastest)

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • Your account and login
  • Your watchlist, watched episodes, ratings, favorites, and custom lists
  • Your display name and any personal info you provided (birth year, gender, country)

This cannot be undone.

Option 2 - Without the app installed

Email us from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete my account", and we'll process the deletion within a few business days.

What gets deleted

Everything listed above is permanently removed from our systems. Data stored only on your own device (if you ever used the app without an account) is not something we have access to or can delete remotely - clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes that.
