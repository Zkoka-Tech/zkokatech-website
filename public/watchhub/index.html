WatchHub

Track what you watch - shows and movies, all in one place.

WatchHub is a personal TV show and movie tracker. Keep a watchlist, mark episodes as you watch them, rate what you've seen, and pick up new episodes as they air - fully usable offline, with optional account sign-in to sync across your devices.

Features

  • Track shows and movies you're watching, want to watch, or have finished
  • Per-episode watch tracking, with automatic "watch next" suggestions
  • Ratings, favorites, and custom lists
  • Works fully offline - sign in only if you want cross-device sync
