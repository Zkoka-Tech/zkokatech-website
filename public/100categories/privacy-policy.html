Privacy Policy for 100 Categories

Last updated: [October 1, 2026]

100 Categories (“the App”) is developed by ZkokaTech. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts and no sign-up, and it never asks for your name, email, phone number or contacts. Team names you type and your game history are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address
  • device information (model, operating system, language)
  • information about how you interact with ads, and crash or diagnostic data

Google uses this information as described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

In-app purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The App only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Game content

The App downloads category lists from our server (hosted by Supabase). These requests contain no personal information about you.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data stored on your device

Your game history, the cached category lists and your purchase status are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
